# Gotcha Force

## What does randomization do to this game?

Story mode becomes an Archipelago world. Winning story missions sends
checks, the game's own milestone rewards (recruiting a GF Commander,
GF Energy upgrades, the Gotcha Box, the Death Crystals and more) are
extra checks, and — with the default settings — **the Gotcha Borg
gacha itself becomes checks**: whenever the gacha pops after a battle,
it sends a check instead of handing you a borg. Every enemy type you
can meet in your seed has its own gacha checks, so hunting down new
enemies is how you collect.

What you receive from the multiworld:

* **Progressive Story Keys** — your missions are sorted by story
  order and split into batches; each key opens the next batch (how
  many keys, and even zero, is up to your YAML).
* **Progressive GF Energy Cap** — raises your deploy budget. Your GF
  Energy is always full: no farming needed.
* **Area Access** (optional) — missions in a map zone only appear once
  you have its access item.
* **Gotcha Box**, **Progressive G Red** (G Red, then Neo G Red),
  **Death Crystals**, **GF Commanders** and their upgrades, and the
  **Gotcha Borgs** themselves — whole, or split into their pieces.
  If you would rather not wait for the Gotcha Box, the Gotcha Box
  option gives it to you from the start.

Missions that put a partner at Kou's side need that partner. A mission
with a fixed partner ("Cooperate with Usagi!") needs that commander; a
mission where you pick your partner needs at least one recruited
commander who is not the rival of that mission; missions Kou plays
alone need nobody. A mission stays hidden on the map until you have a
partner it accepts, and the client's Tracker tab tells you who is
missing.

Enemies can be randomized too (including their AI sharpness and
levels, boss identities, rival commanders' signature borgs and even
your ally partners' teams), and every mission always has the same
line-up within one seed, no matter how many times you retry it.

## What is the goal?

Win one specific mission: the **Cosmic Dragon**, the **Galactic
Emperor**, the **Dark Knight**, a mission of your choice (goal:
custom) or one picked by the seed (goal: random_mission). The goal
mission unlocks by collecting **Death Crystals** (how many is up to
your YAML), and any mission that comes later than it in the story is
left out of the game entirely.

Which missions fill the road to the goal is also up to your YAML
(Mission Pool): every story mission, only the main-story experience,
or a custom set — pick how many missions you want and pin any you
insist on, and the seed draws the rest.

## Which items can be in another player's world?

All of them. Nothing is written to your disc: the client applies
everything live to the running game.

## What does another world's item look like in Gotcha Force?

The client announces received items in its window. In-game, borgs
appear in your collection, commanders join instantly, and your GF
Energy cap jumps the moment the item arrives. When you earn a check
from the gacha, the full "You got a Gotcha Borg!" animation plays as
your in-game feedback.
